# Research guide continuation: 180

Date: 2026-09-01. Scope: public-web editorial candidate only; no provider,
registry, collection, entitlement, runtime, or deployment change.

## Selection and evidence

This continuation converts twenty existing publisher-verified catalogue identities
from summary-only orientations into six-section bilingual reader guides. It keeps
the library at 200 source identities and raises guide coverage from 160 to 180.
Primary DOI landing pages and public source descriptions were checked. Where a
public abstract was available, the guide retains its stated subject and design;
where only bibliographic material was available, the guide is limited to the
title-defined object, required inputs, and non-extrapolation boundary. No
numerical result, table-level claim, causal conclusion, or trading implementation
is inferred beyond public source descriptions.

The batch covers four market-microstructure, seven corporate-fundamentals and
governance, three China/Hong Kong institutional-market, four alternative-data or
attention, and two asset-pricing records. Every guide separates question, design,
data/timing, interpretation, replication checks, and scope boundary. Existing
catalogue identities, original titles, author information, source URLs, related
material selections, topics, and reading paths are unchanged.
