# Research guide completion: 200

Date: 2026-09-02. Scope: `public-web` editorial candidate only. No provider,
registry, collection, entitlement, runtime, or deployment behavior changes.

## Current coverage

The library retains exactly 200 distinct source identities and now has a reader
guide for every identity. The final batch completes twenty previously
summary-only orientations. The final shape is 199 six-section guides plus the
pre-existing four-section Dechow/Dichev abstract-based orientation.

Each completed guide separates research question, design, data and timing,
interpretation, replication checks, and scope boundary. It is tied to its DOI,
official archive, publisher record, or official exchange archive. The guide
stays within public source descriptions and registered bibliographic material:
it does not invent table results, numerical estimates, causal conclusions,
implementation details, current-market claims, or trading rules.

The batch covers official exchange statistics, asset pricing and multiple
testing, resampling and machine learning methods, high-frequency measurement,
corporate-finance theory, public-information and filing text, term structure,
open-economy and currency evidence, and macro-financial transmission. Original
identities, titles, authors, source URLs, topics, related-material selections,
routes, and core reading journeys are unchanged.

## Editorial navigation

`researchComparisons200.js` adds sixteen explicit, bilingual comparison pairs,
bringing the library to 101 authored comparison pairs. Every newly completed
guide has at least one authored comparison. These links contrast scope,
definitions, inputs, or methods; they do not assert citation, agreement,
replication, evidence ranking, or investment relevance. The old automatic
summary-only fallback was removed because no public record is summary-only.
