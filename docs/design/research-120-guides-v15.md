### Definition safeguards

- SRISK follows this ESRB edition's 22-period / market-decline-beyond-10% event; no substitution of a different horizon/threshold from another implementation.
- Diebold/Rudebusch/Aruoba uses short-minus-long slope, not the opposite common term-spread sign.
- Blockchain Economics concerns fault tolerance, resource efficiency and full transferability, not the popular scalability trilemma.
- Wildcat Stablecoins uses the 2023 published article while keeping the 2021 bibliography identity; Optimal Execution uses the 1999 draft while retaining the 2001 citation.
- The long-memory arXiv v2 header dates to July 2004, while the recompiled body says November 2024. That is not a refreshed sample; version/header and historical observations remain distinct.
- HFT price-discovery observations do not supply the unobserved no-HFT counterfactual.
- Policy-shock content is bounded to the introduction's identification framework; neither its later VAR estimates nor today's policy institutions are represented as checked.
